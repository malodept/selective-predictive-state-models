## Interpretation

- Context-only routing is already strong on hard long-horizon shifts.
- Adding observable latent-state features is most useful on `3-block, H=72`, especially at moderate compute costs.
- Candidate-mined geometry is not the right signal; observable latent state complexity is more promising.
- The paper should frame the strongest result as latent-state-aware value-of-computation, not merely geometry-aware routing.
